# Cozy Christmas — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 10 November 2026*

This policy explains what information the mobile game **Cozy Christmas**
(Android and iOS) handles, why, and what choices you have. Cozy Christmas is
made by **pbokros** ("we"). To contact us, open an issue at
https://github.com/pbokros/cozy-christmas-privacy/issues.

## The short version

- The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or any other
  personal details.
- Your progress is saved only on your device. We do not run servers and we
  never receive your game data.
- The game shows optional video ads from **Google AdMob**, only when you
  choose to watch one. Google may collect device information to show and
  measure those ads, as described below.
- In-app purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play** or the **Apple App
  Store**. We never see your payment details.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and the list of items you have
unlocked or bought in a file inside the app's private storage on your device.
This file is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the game turns off Android's
automatic cloud backup for it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Cozy Christmas offers two optional rewarded ads: a gift box in the village and
a "double it" bonus when you come back to the game. There are no banner ads, no
ads between screens, and no ad ever plays unless you tap to watch it.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google. When the game
requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with
Google:

- device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
- your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
- information about how you interact with ads (for example, whether you
  watched or tapped one);
- diagnostic and performance information about the ad SDK.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you
see the same ad, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve its services. Google acts
under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google
uses information from apps that use its services is explained at
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

**Your choices**

- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland,
  the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads
  are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the game under
  **Settings → Privacy options**.
- On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's
  settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
- On iOS the game does not ask for permission to track you, so ads are not
  personalised using your device's advertising identifier.
- You never have to watch an ad. Skipping them only means skipping the
  optional bonuses.

## In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play or the Apple App Store, under their
terms and privacy policies. We do not receive or store your name, email,
payment card or billing address. The store tells the game which item was
bought so it can be unlocked, and that is recorded only in the save file on
your device. "Restore purchases" asks the store again which items your account
owns.

## Children

Cozy Christmas is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. The game itself collects no
personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal
information through the game, contact us and we will help.

## Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to keep, hand
over or delete. Your game data stays on your device until you uninstall the
game. For data collected by Google for advertising, you can use the choices
above or contact Google through its privacy policy. If you live in the EU, the
UK or another region with data-protection rights, you may also contact us
through the link below or complain to your local data-protection authority.

## Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we will update this page
and its effective date before the change ships.
